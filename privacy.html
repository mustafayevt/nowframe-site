Privacy Policy

Last updated October 25, 2026

The short version

  • Nowframe edits, previews and exports on your iPhone. Your projects, photos, videos and audio are not uploaded to us or to anyone else.
  • There is no account. We don’t ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • There is no advertising and no tracking.
  • Face and body analysis runs on your device only, and only after you turn it on.
  • The only thing the app sends off your device is your Nowframe Pro purchase record, with an anonymous identifier, to our subscription provider RevenueCat.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details.

Who we are

Nowframe is made by [Legal entity name], [Registered address] (“we”, “us”). You can reach us at mustafayev.t (at) outlook.com.

Your media stays on your device

Nowframe works on the photos, videos and audio that you choose. The app copies what you select into its own storage on your device so that your projects keep working, and it edits, previews and exports on the device. We don’t receive your media, projects, exports or captions, and we can’t see them.

When you export, the file is saved to your Photos library or handed to the share sheet you choose. What happens after that depends on where you share it, and is covered by that service’s policy.

What we collect

Almost nothing, and nothing that identifies you. We don’t run a server for the app, and the app contains no advertising, attribution or crash-reporting tools. The one exception is the purchase and subscription record described under “Purchases and subscription analytics”. It’s tied to a random identifier, not to your name, email or Apple Account, and it goes to RevenueCat, a service provider that works for us.

What stays on your device

Nowframe keeps the following on your device only:

  • your projects, imported media copies, caches and exports;
  • your settings and choices, such as your appearance setting and your consent answers;
  • your display name, if you set one in the Me tab (it’s never sent anywhere);
  • the results of face and body analysis, if you allowed it;
  • a small count of how many free monthly uses of some tools you’ve had, and a cached copy of whether Nowframe Pro is active. These sit in the iOS Keychain, contain no personal information, and may survive deleting and reinstalling the app.

Deleting Nowframe removes your projects and other app data from the device. We don’t back up your projects, so keep copies of important footage. Your own iPhone or iCloud backup may include them, depending on your settings.

Faces and bodies

Some tools (retouch, reshape, makeup, face props and body effects) need to find faces and bodies in your photos and videos. Before the first use, Nowframe explains this and asks you to agree. If you agree:

  • analysis runs only on your device, with Apple’s on-device Vision technology;
  • faces are only located, never identified or compared with anyone;
  • the results are stored on your device and used only to apply the effect you chose;
  • you can turn this off and delete the stored analysis data at any time in Me > Privacy.

If you choose “Not now”, those tools stay off and the rest of the app works as before.

Purchases and subscription analytics

Nowframe uses RevenueCat, Inc. (“RevenueCat”) to check that purchases are valid and to help us understand how many people try and keep Nowframe Pro. When the app starts, and when you view plans or buy, it sends RevenueCat:

  • an anonymous random identifier created by the app;
  • the App Store purchase information for Nowframe Pro: the product, dates, price and currency, store country, and whether it’s a trial or a renewal;
  • where in the app the plans screen was opened (for example, after an export), which plan you picked, and your plan state (free, trial, subscribed or lifetime);
  • basic technical information: app and iOS version, device type and language.

Like any internet request, it also reaches RevenueCat with your IP address. This data isn’t linked to your name, email or Apple Account, and isn’t used to track you across other companies’ apps and websites. RevenueCat acts as our service provider and is based in the United States. Your photos, videos, audio, captions, projects and edits are never sent to RevenueCat or to us. The free features keep working if the connection to RevenueCat fails.

You can ask us to delete the data tied to your identifier by writing to us at the address above.

Apple services the app uses

Some features rely on services built into iOS. Apple runs them, and Apple’s own terms and privacy policy apply.

  • App Store purchases. Apple handles payment and tells the app whether Pro is active on your Apple Account. We don’t receive your name, email or card. Apple may give us aggregated sales reports that don’t identify you.
  • iCloud Photos. If a photo or video you pick is stored in iCloud rather than on the device, iOS can download it for you. You can limit this on cellular in Me > Privacy.
  • Speech recognition and translation. Captions and caption translation run on your device. The first time you use a language, iOS may download a model from Apple.
  • Apple’s on-device language model, on iPhones that support it, for writing help. It runs on the device.

Support and diagnostics

If you contact support from the app, your mail app opens with your device model, iOS version and app version in the message. If you choose to attach a diagnostics report, it’s cleaned first to remove file paths, file names, project names and email addresses, and you can read it before you send it. We receive only what you send, use it only to answer you and fix problems, and keep it only as long as that takes. You can ask us to delete your messages at any time.

Sharing

We don’t sell or rent personal data, and we don’t allow tracking. The only data that leaves your device is the purchase record and anonymous identifier we share with RevenueCat as our service provider, and whatever you choose to send to support.

Children

Nowframe is a general-audience video editor and isn’t directed at children under 13, or under the age of digital consent where you live. We don’t knowingly collect personal data from anyone.

Your rights

Because the app doesn’t collect personal data that identifies you, there is usually little for us to access, correct, delete or export. If you wrote to us, you can ask what we hold from that exchange and ask us to delete it. Depending on where you live (for example the EU or UK under the GDPR, or California under the CCPA and CPRA), you have the right to access, correct and delete your personal data, to object to or restrict its use, and to complain to your data protection authority. Write to us at the address above.

Security

Your media and projects stay on your device and are protected by its own security: your passcode, Face ID or Touch ID, and encryption. Data sent to RevenueCat travels over an encrypted connection.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Nowframe handles data, we’ll update this page and the date at the top, and tell you in the app before a change that matters to you takes effect. We would ask for your consent before any new feature sends data off your device.
