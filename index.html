Questions

Is Nowframe really free?

Yes. Unlimited projects, the whole timeline, auto captions, text, music, voiceover and 1080p export with no watermark are free. Pro is optional and adds the extras listed above.

Do I need an account?

No. There is nothing to sign up for. If you buy Pro, it’s tied to your Apple Account through the App Store, and Restore purchases brings it back on a new iPhone.

Where are my projects stored?

On your iPhone, inside Nowframe. They aren’t uploaded anywhere, so keep copies of footage that matters. Your own iPhone or iCloud backup may include them, depending on your settings.

Which iPhones does it run on?

Any iPhone with iOS 17 or later. HDR export, smooth slow motion and some on-device tools depend on your iPhone model.

What happens to my projects if Pro ends?

They stay yours and stay editable. When you export, you can get Pro again or export without the Pro items, and 4K and 60 fps fall back to 1080p at 30 fps.

How do I cancel a subscription?

On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Deleting the app doesn’t cancel it. A canceled plan stays active until the end of the period you paid for.
