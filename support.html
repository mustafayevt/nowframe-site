How do I restore my purchase?

Open the Me tab and tap Restore purchases under Nowframe Pro, or tap it on the plans screen. Make sure you’re signed in to the same Apple Account you bought Pro with. Restore also brings Pro to a new iPhone or back after a reinstall.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Apple manages subscriptions. On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose Nowframe Pro and tap Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the period to avoid the next charge. Deleting the app doesn’t cancel it. Pro stays active until the end of the period you paid for, and your projects stay editable after that.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases go through Apple, so Apple handles refunds. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with your Apple Account and choose the purchase.

My export failed or stopped

Keep Nowframe open while it exports. If you leave the app, the export keeps going for a short while, then pauses until you come back.

Check your free storage in iOS Settings > General > iPhone Storage. A long 4K export needs room for the finished file. In Nowframe, Me > Manage storage shows what the app uses and lets you clear caches.

If a clip is stored in iCloud, it has to finish downloading first. Try again on Wi-Fi.

2K, 4K, 50 and 60 fps and HDR need Nowframe Pro, and HDR needs an iPhone that supports it. If an export still fails, try 1080p, then write to us with a diagnostics report attached.

Where are my projects stored?

On your iPhone, inside Nowframe. They’re never uploaded, so we can’t see or recover them. A project you delete goes to Trash in the Me tab first, but deleting the app deletes all of them. Your exported videos and photos are saved to the Photos app. To keep a project safe, export it, and keep your iPhone backed up.

Nowframe can’t see my photos

Open iOS Settings > Apps > Nowframe > Photos and choose Full Access, or add the items you want under Limited Access.

Captions aren’t being made

Captions are made on your iPhone with Apple’s speech recognition. Allow Speech Recognition for Nowframe in iOS Settings. The first time you use a language, iOS may need to download it, so stay online for that first run.

Do I need an account?

No. Nowframe has no account. Pro is tied to your Apple Account through the App Store.
